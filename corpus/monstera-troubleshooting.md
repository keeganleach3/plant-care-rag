## Yellowing leaves or black spots on Monstera

Yellowing and black spots on a Monstera are most often root rot. Root rot is the biggest issue for small plants held in high humidity without enough airflow.

## No new growth or small leaves on Monstera

No new growth or undersized leaves on a Monstera almost always means not enough fertilizer and not enough light. Variegated Monstera in particular want a lot of light. The questions to ask first: how consistent is the watering and fertilizing routine, what light is the plant under, and how far from it is the plant.
