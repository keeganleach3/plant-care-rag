## Every grow area has a light fan running

Every cabinet and grow area has a light fan to fight fungus and mold. It does not need to be strong: leaves should not be whipping around. The test is slight movement in some leaves, or a little movement in the fabric wall of a grow tent. That is enough to keep the air from going still and stale around the plants, which is what causes fungal problems. When fungus shows up anywhere, airflow is one of the first things to check.

## Vent timing works like lid timing

Opening the vents on a dome follows the same rule as lifting the lid: open a little more over time, slowly, as part of the humidity step-down. There is no separate vent schedule.
