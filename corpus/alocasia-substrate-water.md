## Alocasia grow in a wick-fed semi-hydro setup

Alocasia here are kept in a semi-hydro style setup: the plant stays in the tree fern fiber and perlite mix, with a simple cotton wick strung through the bottom of the pot down into a water reservoir underneath. Pon or LECA work too, and there are many ready-made wick pots sold online; the cotton string is just the simplest version. The point is consistent moisture, which Alocasia need and an irregular hand-watering schedule does not give them.

## Topping up an Alocasia wick reservoir

Refill the reservoir when it gets low, around half an inch of water left, so the cotton wick never dries out and stays moist all the way up into the pot.
