## Brown tips and slow growth on Anthurium

Brown tips and slow growth are the Anthurium questions customers ask most, and they are normally a humidity and watering issue. The common mistake is giving an Anthurium too little light and treating it as fussily as an Alocasia. Anthurium are not as dramatic as Alocasia and hold up well; the one thing to guard is never letting them dry out completely.
