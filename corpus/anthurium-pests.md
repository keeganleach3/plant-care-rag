## No Anthurium-specific pests or fungus

There are no pests or fungal problems specific to Anthurium. They are treated the same as a Monstera: manual wiping, consistent spraying, and regular checking, following the general thrips, spider mite, and mealybug protocols. Anthurium are watched less closely for pests than Alocasia, which is the one genus most prone to both pests and fungus, but every plant still gets checked.
