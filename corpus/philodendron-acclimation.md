## Which Philodendron this covers

A variety of Philodendron are grown from tissue culture here. The most common is Philodendron Caramel Marble, along with its pink form, sold as Pink Caramel Marble or Strawberry Philodendron, which is a slightly separate plant. These are very slow growers.

## Philodendron are hardy and treated like Monstera

Philodendron are treated much the same as Monstera. Both are hardy. Philodendron can dry out, can take low humidity, and are resilient to changes in conditions.

## Philodendron humidity step-down

Philodendron sit between Monstera and Alocasia on humidity step-down: more time than a Monstera's three weeks, less than an Alocasia's four to five. They get a little longer than a Monstera specifically because their roots are thinner.

## Philodendron root the slowest

Philodendron are the slowest rooters of the four genera. Monstera are fastest, Anthurium and Alocasia are second, Philodendron last. Philodendron also do well climbing.
