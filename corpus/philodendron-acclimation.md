## Which Philodendron this covers

A variety of Philodendron are grown from tissue culture here. The most common is Philodendron Caramel Marble and its related forms [CONFIRM: "strawberry caramel marble" in the transcript; is that a separate cultivar, such as Strawberry Shake?]. These are very slow growers.

## Philodendron are hardy and treated like Monstera

Philodendron are treated much the same as Monstera. Both are hardy. Philodendron can dry out, can take low humidity, and are resilient to changes in conditions.

## Philodendron humidity step-down

Philodendron sit between Monstera and Alocasia on humidity step-down: more time than a Monstera, less than an Alocasia [CONFIRM: you also said Philodendron get the same step-down schedule as a Monstera; which is it?].

## Philodendron root the slowest

Philodendron are the slowest rooters of the four genera. Monstera are fastest, Anthurium and Alocasia are second, Philodendron last. Philodendron also do well climbing.
