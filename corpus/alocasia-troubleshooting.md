## Droopy or dying leaves on a small Alocasia

Alocasia are dramatic: droopy leaves and leaves dying one after another are the most common customer complaint, and it is usually an overreaction. A small Alocasia normally cannot hold more than a couple of leaves. While small, it will typically grow one new leaf while the older leaf dies at the same time, so the plant sits at one leaf. Give it time; as the root system gets stronger and the plant gets bigger it will hold more leaves.

## Alocasia need consistent water, fertilizer, and light

Alocasia are heavy feeders and need a lot of nutrients, plus consistent light. They also need very consistent watering and do not tolerate an irregular schedule the way a Monstera might. A self-watering setup is highly recommended for Alocasia to keep the watering consistent.
