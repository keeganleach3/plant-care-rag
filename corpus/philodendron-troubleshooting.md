## Slow growth on Philodendron is usually normal

Slow growth is the Philodendron question customers ask most, and most of the time it is simply how the plant grows: Philodendron, and Caramel Marble types especially, are very slow growers. It is the main thing customers get confused about with Philodendron and Anthurium.
