## Established plants: at least 300 PPFD for 12 to 14 hours

Once past acclimation, all of these aroids, and variegated forms especially, want a lot of light. Grow lights here sit very close to the plants and run twelve to fourteen hours a day, with the plants getting at least 300 PPFD for the most part. Acclimating plantlets use a lower 150 to 200 PPFD target; see the acclimation protocol.

## Light intensity drops off fast with distance

Usable light drops off with distance from the fixture far faster than people expect, and a plant twelve inches from a light gets a small fraction of what it gets at six inches. It also drops toward the sides of a fixture compared with directly under its center. Cheaper fixtures and cheaper LED nodes make both effects worse.

## Cheap grow lights work only up close

Cheap grow lights are not inherently wrong. Barrina T5 fixtures are very popular with customers but very weak, and off-brand lights give roughly what you pay for. Plants under them need to be kept very close to the fixture.
