## All Alocasia get the same treatment

No single Alocasia is grown here more than the others. Some are more sensitive and slower-growing than others, but every Alocasia gets the same materials and protocol. An extremely expensive plant gets more consistent watching and care, but nothing about the substrate, water, or schedule changes.

## Alocasia need a slower humidity step-down than Monstera

Alocasia get an extra five to seven days at 100 percent humidity compared with Monstera. Then, instead of reaching 65 percent humidity in about two weeks the way a Monstera does, an Alocasia takes three to four weeks to reach 65 percent. Once acclimated, an Alocasia is still not moved straight into ambient room humidity the way a Monstera can be.

## Leaf drop during Alocasia acclimation is normal

Alocasia lose leaves during acclimation very often. Most of the time new leaves replace the ones that drop. If the plant goes dormant instead, give it one to two months, sometimes longer, to show signs of growth or improvement before giving up, as long as the rhizome at the center stays firm.
