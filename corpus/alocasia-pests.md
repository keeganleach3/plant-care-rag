## Leaf spot on Alocasia: what it looks like

Fungal leaf spot on Alocasia shows as black or brown spots on random parts of the leaf with rings around each spot. It is the most common fungal problem on this genus.

## Leaf spot on Alocasia: what to do

Cut off the affected leaves immediately, even if it is the plant's only leaf, and spray with a copper fungicide. A fungicide can also be applied while watering. If leaf spot keeps recurring, add more airflow to the setup. Isolate the plant if it sits near other Alocasia, and treat the surrounding plants with a fungicide as well, exactly as you would for a pest outbreak.

## Alocasia attract more pests and get treated longer

Alocasia tend to attract more pests than other genera. When pests are found on an Alocasia, treat for longer than you would treat a Monstera, and keep watching the plant closely afterward to confirm nothing new has arrived. The pest protocols themselves (thrips, spider mites, mealybugs) are the same as for other aroids.
